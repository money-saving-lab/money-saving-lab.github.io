Before you buy

A short checklist. Ticks stay on this device only.

Label words

Plain-language notes on terms that appear on these listings. These explain the words, not what a product will do for you.

AHA / BHA / PHAFamilies of exfoliating acids. On these listings: citric acid (AHA) and white willow bark (called a natural BHA).
CeramideA type of lipid that is also found naturally in skin. Often written as "ceramide NP" on ingredient lists.
CicaShort for centella asiatica, a plant. Madecassoside is one of its components.
HeartleafHouttuynia cordata, a plant widely used in Korean skincare.
NiacinamideA form of vitamin B3.
PDRNPolydeoxyribonucleotide, a DNA-derived ingredient. These listings say it comes from salmon DNA; one names rice bran.
RetinalRetinaldehyde, a vitamin A ingredient. Not the same word as retinol.
TXATranexamic acid.
Non-comedogenicA claim made by the brand, usually based on its own testing. There is no single official standard for it.
